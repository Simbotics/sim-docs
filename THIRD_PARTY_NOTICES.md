[`static/teavm`](./static/teavm). These files come from or are generated by the
[TeaVM-Javac project](https://github.com/konsoletyper/teavm-javac) and the
[TeaVM project](https://teavm.org/).

TeaVM-Javac is licensed under the
[Apache License 2.0](https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0). Its compiler
WebAssembly includes components from OpenJDK, which are licensed under the
[GNU General Public License, version 2, with the Classpath Exception](https://openjdk.org/legal/gplv2+ce.html).

The upstream project and license notice state that OpenJDK source is compiled
into the distributed WebAssembly output. The corresponding upstream source and
build instructions are available from the TeaVM-Javac repository. Additional
asset provenance and checksums are recorded in
[`static/teavm/README.md`](./static/teavm/README.md).
